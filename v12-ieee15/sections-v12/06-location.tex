\textbf{Controlled edits.} The cascade reached a correct outcome on \casCorrect\% of the main seed's trials, deletions included (twelve operators of \nPerOp{} each) (95\% CI \casLo--\casHi), against \qeCorrect\% for exact quotes, \ddCorrect\% for diff drift and \lnCorrect\% for fixed lines; it placed \smCtlCasPlaceK{} of the \smCtlCasPlaceN{} surviving anchors correctly (\smCtlCasPlacePct\%, Table~\ref{tab:safety}, against \smCtlQuotePlacePct\%, \smCtlDriftPlacePct\% and \smCtlLinePlacePct\%); on the replication seed it reached \casRep\%. The conventional policies fail by giving up, losing every edited anchor and, for fixed lines, every shifted one. Cascade v1 reached \voneCorrect\%: it recovered slightly more cross-file moves but attached \voneWrongRewrite\% of rewritten and \voneWrongDeleteBlock\% of deleted anchors to look-alike code elsewhere, against \casWrongRewrite\% and \casWrongDeleteBlock\% for the final tier order. The contextual tracker is same-file in scope, so it loses every cross-file move; within files it was slightly more accurate than the cascade. The controlled mix weights twelve operators equally and is not an estimate for natural revisions.

\textbf{Real histories.} On the uniform samples most targets are untouched or shifted, and the cascade reached \rUCas\% pooled over nine projects. On the edit-targeted samples it reached \rOssTCas\% on the public and \rOwnTCas\% on our own projects, and Cascade v1, at its lower threshold, did better (\rTVone\% against \rTCas\% pooled). By reference class the cascade recovered every in-place and shifted target, \rClsMovedFileCas\% of cross-file moves and \rClsEditedCas\% of edited statements, whereas the three conventional policies recover no edited statement by construction. The contextual tracker placed more surviving targets than the cascade (\smRealTrkPlacePct\% against \smRealCasPlacePct\%; Table~\ref{tab:safety}), most of the difference on edited code.

\textbf{Threshold and design.} At the default $\theta=0.85$ the cascade relocated \thetaEditedEightyFive\% of edited real-history statements and reported \thetaGoneEightyFive\% of deleted ones as removed; at $\theta=0.50$ the figures were \thetaEditedFifty\% and \thetaGoneFifty\%. No threshold is best for both. In a factorial ablation over tier order, search scope and threshold, searching the whole file instead of the local window lowered the share of deletions reported removed by \wpaScopeGoneInt{} points on our projects and \wpaScopeGonePub{} on public ones, and running cross-file search before local fuzzy search raised missed re-checks by \wpaOrderMissInt{} and \wpaOrderMissPub{} points, so both deployed choices are supported on safety grounds. Leave-one-project-out calibration for accuracy selected $\theta=\wpaLopoTheta$ in all \wpaLopoFolds{} folds; the calibrated rows of Tables~\ref{tab:locator} and~\ref{tab:external} use this value. Its placement gain has a cost the miss and silent rates hide: with the guards at $\theta=\wpaLopoTheta$, \wiRealCalGiGoneKeptK{} of \wiRealCalGiGoneKeptN{} deleted real-history targets (deployed: \wiRealDepGoneKeptK{}) and \wiHumCalGiGoneKeptK{} of \wiHumCalGiGoneKeptN{} human-annotated ones (deployed: \wiHumDepGoneKeptK{}) were relocated onto other code, requesting a re-check at the wrong place (Misdir). Calibration for safety did not transfer: of \wpaConstrFeasible{} threshold choices that met limits on missed re-checks and on deleted code reported present in our own projects, \wpaConstrViolate{} violated at least one of their own limits on the public projects.

\textbf{Human-annotated benchmark.} On regions annotated by people the deployed cascade found the correct line, or correctly reported the region gone, for \cExtHumDepAcc\% (placement on surviving regions \wiHumDepPlace\%), the contextual tracker for \cExtHumTrkAcc\% and CodeMapper for \cExtHumCmAcc\% (Table~\ref{tab:external}). CodeMapper recovered \cExtHumCmChars\% of the annotated character regions exactly, the cascade \cExtHumDepChars\%; outside Python the cascade reached \cExtHumNpDepAcc\% and CodeMapper \cExtHumNpCmAcc\%. Our runs of the pinned CodeMapper did not reproduce its published figures exactly (\cRepAObserved{} against \cRepAPublished{} exact regions of \cRepAN{} in the first set); we report our own runs.

\textbf{Student-authored repositories.} The deployed cascade reached a correct outcome on \hTwoDepAcc\% of the targets (commit-pair cluster interval \hTwoDepAccLo--\hTwoDepAccHi\%). The contextual tracker reached \hTwoTrkAcc\% when given Git's rename detection, as in the held-out run (deployed minus tracker \hTwoDiffLo{} to \hTwoDiffHi{} points), leading in \hTwoTrkLeadRepos{} of \hTwoRepos{} repositories, and \wiStuTrQAcc\% when given the original path only, as every locator in Table~\ref{tab:locator} is, the difference being its \wiStuTrRenamed{} placements on renamed files. On edit-targeted observations it leads under either rule (\hTwoTrkTgtAcc\% and \wiStuTgtTrQAcc\%, against \hTwoDepTgtAcc\%).

\textbf{Language models.} On the edit-targeted open-source rows \wjModel{} placed \wjPubSelfPlace\% of the surviving targets and \wjNemoModel{} \wjNemoPubSelfPlace\%, against \wjPubCmQPlace\% for CodeMapper and \wjPubDepPlace\% for the deployed cascade; on the student rows the figures were \wjStuSelfPlace\%, \wjNemoStuSelfPlace\%, \wjStuCmQPlace\% and \wjStuDepPlace\%. On the human-annotated regions CodeMapper placed more (\wjHumCmQPlace\%, against \wjHumSelfPlace\% and \wjNemoHumSelfPlace\%).

\textbf{Answer to RQ1.} At its deployed threshold the cascade is not a competitive locator: a contextual tracker, a dedicated region mapper and two general language models each re-find edited code more often, and its fixed-height windows recover region boundaries poorly. Calibrated and guarded, it is competitive on Python: it placed \wiStuCalGiPlace\% of the surviving student targets against \wiStuCmQPlace\% for CodeMapper and \wiStuTrQPlace\% for the tracker, and \wiRealCalGiPlace\% of the real-history targets against \wiRealCmQPlace\% for CodeMapper, but it then keeps more deleted code, and on the multi-language human-annotated regions CodeMapper still placed more (\wiHumCmQPlace\% against \wiHumCalGiPlace\%). The ranking of locators depends on the population and the threshold. A deployment should therefore be free to choose or replace the locator, which is safe only if the safety argument does not depend on it.
