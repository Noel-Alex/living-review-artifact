\newcommand{\vAbstract}{%
Review comments must follow revised code, and a changed location must not be mistaken for a resolved concern. We present \sys{}, the resubmission model of a capstone review portal, and evaluate its safety layer: change labels, indentation and copy guards, a rule that keeps the reviewed quotation, and issue states that return an issue to its supervisor whenever an anchor changed or disappeared. The portal's four-tier content cascade is not a competitive locator: a contextual line tracker re-finds edited code more often, and on a human-annotated multi-language benchmark the region mapper CodeMapper handled \cExtHumCmAcc\% of regions correctly against \cExtHumDepAcc\%. Safety failures were rare but present: on real histories \smRealCasMissPct\% of changed or deleted targets were not returned for re-check, all through a pre-existing identical copy, and on constructed anchors with an identical copy nearby the deployed cascade missed \bDupMissDeployed\% of needed re-checks (\bDupMissAG\% with a copy guard). Three guards cut missed re-checks from \hTwoDepMiss\% to \hTwoAllMiss\% on held-out student-authored repositories. The layer does not depend on the locator. Over CodeMapper on a held-out stress seed it cut missed re-checks from \wiFrCmQMiss\% to \wiFrCmGiMiss\% and silent misplacements from \wiFrCmQSilent\% to \wiFrCmGiSilent\%, with placement unchanged. With the portal's own language model as the locator, the model's judgment of change left \wjPubSelfMissK{} of \wjPubSelfMissN{} needed re-checks unrequested on open-source histories, and the layer left \wjPubGiMissK{}. Both gains cost more requests on preserved code. Across ten resubmissions, keeping the reviewed quotation cut the changes since review that the latest request failed to signal from \wiChIOffMissLastTenK{} to \wiChIOffIdMissLastTenK{}, the changes no hop had detected. At the default threshold the cascade relocated only \thetaEditedEightyFive\% of edited real-history statements but reported \thetaGoneEightyFive\% of deleted ones as removed, and safety-constrained thresholds did not transfer between project groups. \newcommand{\vConclusion}{%
\sys{} separates where a review concern now points from whether it has been settled. Its contribution is the safety layer and state contract around the matcher, not the matcher: an independent contextual tracker and the region mapper CodeMapper locate edited code better, and the cascade contributes cross-file recovery. Missed re-checks are rare on real histories and arise from one identifiable mechanism, identical copies of edited code, which guards address on held-out student-authored, human-annotated and stress data at a measurable cost in confirmations. The same labels and window guards made CodeMapper safer without changing where it placed anything, so the locator can be upgraded while the safety argument stays the same. A language model, the one the portal already uses, located edited code well, but its own judgment of change missed re-checks that the same layer then caught. Content alone cannot tell two identical copies apart; the guards decide when to ask, not which copy is right. Keeping the reviewed quotation makes uncertain identity transitions visible across many resubmissions. The similarity threshold is a policy choice between relocating edited code and reporting deleted code honestly, and safety limits chosen on one group of projects did not hold on another. The implemented lifecycle never resolves an issue on its own, but deletion stops an issue from being carried and dismissal memory is a heuristic. Whether carrying feedback forward helps supervisors and students remains a question for a consented classroom study. In order of priority, the next steps are that study on real resubmissions, an independent audit of the syntax-tree reference with the prepared blinded packet, and deployment of the identity rule with the stronger locator in the portal.}
